Finding 1 — The Right Bet
AI is already your #1 time investment — now it needs to pay off
You spent 16 hours on AI learning and setup in 14 days — more than client calls, more than SEO work, more than team management combined. That's not dabbling — that's a deliberate bet. The next step is converting that setup time into hours reclaimed elsewhere. The automations below are exactly that conversion.
Finding 2 — The Drain
You are your own BDR — and it costs you 4–5 hours every week
Manual client outreach consumed 10+ hours across the period: 4 hours calling Jade's leads in a single day, scattered "Call Customers" blocks every afternoon, individual calls to Absolute, Guillen, EVS, Texas Boys, A Plus. Every follow-up is a personal phone call. No sequence, no system, no leverage — just Jason dialing.
Finding 3 — The Gap
Client attention is inconsistent — burst sessions, not steady rhythms
Jade got 4 hours in one burst. Awesome Water got multiple emails with no confirmed response. Absolute Plumbing got one 30-min review. There is no automated weekly touchpoint per client — results happen when you remember to check, not on a system that runs without you. That gap is the first thing to close.
Section 2

What I'm Building

6 automation opportunities from the time audit, ranked by impact: hours reclaimed × alignment with scaling Home Court Growth.

1
AI-Powered Lead Outreach & Follow-Up System
Not started
"New leads get personalized follow-up sequences automatically — I focus on closing, not chasing."
What's Happening Today
4 hours calling Jade's leads in a single day. Multiple "Call Customers" blocks (1–2h each) fill every afternoon. Every follow-up is a manual phone call Jason personally makes — he's running a full BDR function himself with no leverage.
What the Automated Version Looks Like
Lead comes in → AI sends personalized SMS/email intro → follows up at Day 2, 5, 10 with context-aware messages → surfaces hot leads with a one-tap "call now" notification. Jason only picks up the phone for interested prospects.
Big Build ⏱ 4–5 hrs / week reclaimed
2
Weekly Client Performance Digest
Not started
"Every client gets a weekly performance update without me touching it, and my reviews become 10-minute decisions instead of 30-minute briefings."
What's Happening Today
Online Presence Review meetings with Absolute Plumbing, Guillen, and Safe Plumbing are 30-min manual sessions. Sean got a separate 45-min briefing on Mike's Plumbing Maps strategy. No automated reporting pipeline — every update requires Jason to gather data himself.
What the Automated Version Looks Like
Every Friday, an AI agent pulls rank data, GBP impressions, and call tracking → generates plain-English summary emails to each client → Jason gets a 1-page internal digest Monday morning. Reviews shift from "let me look this up" to "here's what changed."
Medium Lift ⏱ 2–3 hrs / week reclaimed
3
AI SEO Blog Drafting Pipeline
Not started
"Blog content for all clients goes live every week without me writing or recording anything."
What's Happening Today
"Blog for EVS Mechanical" (30 min), "record video for JADE V on Mike's Plumbing Maps Pack rankings" (30 min), "Enzo's instagram" (60 min) — SEO content is being created manually per client, per week, by Jason or his team.
What the Automated Version Looks Like
Claude receives weekly keyword targets per client → drafts 2–3 blogs + social captions → drops them into a Google Doc for 5-minute human review → approved content auto-posts via CMS API. The SEO team shifts from creating to reviewing.
Quick Win ⏱ 2–3 hrs / week reclaimed
4
Appointment Setter Daily Activity Digest
Not started
"I know exactly what my appointment setters did today without a meeting or a check-in."
What's Happening Today
A 30-min team meeting every Friday is the primary oversight window. Between meetings, setter performance is a black box. The SEO team review happens at 10:45pm Pacific — late-night oversight that signals timezone friction and reactive management.
What the Automated Version Looks Like
Setters log calls/outcomes in a shared sheet → at 6pm daily, an AI agent generates a summary (calls made, appointments booked, top objections, flags) → sent to Jason's phone as a text. Weekly meeting becomes 10-min strategy, not status report.
Quick Win ⏱ 1.5 hrs / week reclaimed
5
Demo Prep Auto-Generator
Not started
"Every sales demo starts with a custom brief already in my hands — I don't prepare, I just show up."
What's Happening Today
"Demo Prep" appears as a recurring 30-min block before client calls on Apr 2, 9, and 13. Each session is manual — researching the prospect, building talking points, reviewing their current online presence from scratch every time.
What the Automated Version Looks Like
Jason adds a business name to a form → AI pulls GBP data, Maps Pack ranking, competitor analysis, website score, and 3 key gaps → generates a one-page pre-call brief → lands in his inbox 1 hour before the demo. Zero prep time.
Quick Win ⏱ 1 hr / week reclaimed
6
Stripe Subscription Monitoring & Billing Automation
Not started
"Billing issues surface themselves — I'm never manually reconciling subscriptions or chasing payment problems."
What's Happening Today
"Update Stripe Subscriptions" appeared as a 45-min calendar block on Apr 20. Manual subscription management across 10+ clients creates fragile billing state — one missed update means a wrong charge or silent churn.
What the Automated Version Looks Like
Stripe webhook → AI monitors subscription events → flags cancellations, failed payments, or upgrades → sends a daily 3-line summary and auto-handles routine cases (dunning, payment retry). Jason only touches billing for exceptions.
Medium Lift ⏱ 0.5–1 hr / week reclaimed
